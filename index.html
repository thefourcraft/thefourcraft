ABOUT ME

Life is pretty hectic for me with all the different roles I juggle. I'm always on the go!

By day, I serve in the Israel Defense Forces (IDF).
By evening, I step into my role as CISO at AiDock, securing and optimizing digital operations.
By night, I lead as CTO at IMS - Network, shaping the future of gaming and technology.

Beyond my professional work, I'm deeply involved in open-source projects and growing my brand, DF.
I also serve as Chairman of the Israeli Gaming Association, driving the expansion of Israel’s gaming industry. Additionally, I volunteer as CTO at TeGriAi, Israel’s largest gaming community, which has grown to over 260,000 members.

Since the start of the Iron Swords War, I’ve been committed to the digital front, using my expertise to defend Israel and amplify its presence in the online sphere.
